# Dreamz Studios: a film studio inside your own Claude

Dreamz Studios is a film studio that works inside your own Claude: add the Dreamz connector (an MCP server) to Claude, or the Dreamz plugin to Claude Code, and your Claude plans, writes, makes, checks and finishes films, pictures, voices and music with you. Built for Claude (recommended); other AI apps can connect with fewer abilities.

This repository is Dreamz's public listing. The studio itself runs at https://dreamz-studios.com.

## Connect

- **Connector (MCP server, remote):** `https://dreamz-studios.com/mcp` (Streamable HTTP, OAuth sign-in). In Claude: Customize, then Connectors, then Add custom connector. Name: Dreamz Studios. URL: https://dreamz-studios.com/mcp. Press Connect.
- **Claude Code plugin:**
  ```
  /plugin marketplace add https://dreamz-studios.com/portfolio/plugin/marketplace.json
  /plugin install dreamz@dreamz-studios
  ```
- Start here (7 days free): https://dreamz-studios.com/claude/start/

## What it makes

Commercials and product ads, short films and series with consistent characters, music videos, narrated films, pictures, voice-overs and songs, finished in every size for every channel. With Claude Code, your Dreamz Brain remembers you and your clients, and files you drop into your studio folder are sorted into the right client and job.

## How it works

- Every prompt is written and checked for the engine it goes to; every take is looked at before you see it.
- The price comes first and nothing paid runs without your yes.
- Generation runs on your own provider keys (an Anthropic key is required; a video provider such as KIE). Each provider bills you directly; Dreamz adds no markup.
- The Dreamz plan: first 7 days free, then $200 a month; cancel any time.
